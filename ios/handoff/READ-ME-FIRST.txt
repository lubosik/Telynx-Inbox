VICI INBOX - APPLE SETUP
========================

Hi Vin,

There are two files in here:

  1. START-HERE.html   <-- double-click this. It's the guide, with
                           pictures showing exactly where to click.

  2. ViciInbox_VoIP.certSigningRequest
                        <-- don't open this. You just upload it
                            during Task 2 of the guide.

The whole thing takes about 10 minutes.

At the end you'll download a file called voip_services.cer.
Send that one back to Lubosi and you're done.

You will NEVER be asked for your Apple password or a verification
code. Don't share those with anyone.


QUICK VERSION (if you'd rather not open the guide)
--------------------------------------------------

TASK 1 - Register the app
  Go to: developer.apple.com/account/resources/identifiers/list
  Click the blue + button
  Choose "App IDs" -> Continue
  Choose "App" -> Continue
  Description: Vici Inbox
  Bundle ID: select "Explicit", then type exactly:

      com.vicipeptides.inbox

  Scroll the Capabilities list and TICK "Push Notifications"
  Continue -> Register

TASK 2 - Create the calling certificate
  Go to: developer.apple.com/account/resources/certificates/list
  Click the blue + button
  Scroll down to the SERVICES section
  Choose "VoIP Services Certificate" -> Continue
     (NOT "Apple Push Notification service SSL" - wrong one)
  Pick "Vici Inbox" from the dropdown -> Continue
  Click "Choose File" and select ViciInbox_VoIP.certSigningRequest
  Continue -> Download

  Send the downloaded voip_services.cer back to Lubosi.


ONE THING FOR THE CALENDAR
--------------------------
July 2027: this certificate expires after one year. When it does,
incoming calls quietly stop ringing on the phones with no other
warning. Renewing is just Task 2 again, five minutes.
