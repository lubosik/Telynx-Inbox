Vici Inbox — Apple setup

Two tasks in the Apple Developer site. About 10 minutes total.

This lets incoming calls to the Vici business number ring on our iPhones like a normal phone call. Apple only allows the person who owns the developer account to do these two steps, which is why it needs you.

You will never be asked for your Apple password or a verification code. Nobody needs them, and you should not share them with anyone, including us.
One file to have ready. Along with this guide you were sent a file called ViciInbox_VoIP.certSigningRequest. Save it somewhere you can find it, like your Downloads folder. You will upload it in Task 2. It contains no passwords and is safe to keep.

The pictures below are drawings of the Apple screens, made to match what you will see. Apple sometimes changes a word or moves a button slightly, so go by the labels rather than the exact look.

Last step — send that file back.
WhatsApp or email voip_services.cer to Lubosi. That is everything we need.
On a Mac it will be in your Downloads folder. If WhatsApp will not attach it directly, drag it into an email instead.

After this

You will not need to touch the Apple site again. Everything else is handled from our side.

One date for your calendar: July 2027.
Apple expires this certificate after one year. When it lapses, incoming calls quietly stop ringing on the phones with no other warning sign. Renewing is just Task 2 again, five minutes. Worth setting a reminder now.

If something goes wrong

“An App ID with this identifier already exists.” — someone already registered it. Skip Task 1 and go straight to Task 2.

You cannot find VoIP Services Certificate. — keep scrolling. It sits in the Services section, below the Software section, near the bottom.

The dropdown in Task 2 is empty. — Task 1 did not save. Go back and redo it.

Anything else, just send a screenshot and we will sort it out.
