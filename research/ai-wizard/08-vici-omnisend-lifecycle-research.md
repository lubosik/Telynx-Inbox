# 08 — Vici lifecycle, Omnisend, VIP, and referral research

**Research snapshot:** 1 October 2026
**Status:** Evidence library and decision memo, not a campaign, provider approval, legal opinion, or instruction to send.
**Scope:** US-first; individual recipients still require country/local-time review. No production writes or messages were made for this research.

## Executive decision

Vici has two distinct populations, not one large SMS list:

1. **First-purchase prospects:** WordPress accounts without a paid WooCommerce order. The current audit found 706 consumer accounts in the rolling 30-day cohort. Of those, 195 have valid stored phones; 187 of the 195 have never placed any WooCommerce order, and eight have only cancelled/on-hold attempts. All 195 exist in Omnisend by email. This is an acquisition/onboarding and friction-diagnosis problem, not a VIP treatment program.
2. **VIP customers:** the existing LUKO rule is at least three distinct paid orders **and** $500 lifetime spend. This is a retention and service-quality population. Do not merge the zero-order cohort into it or imply that possessing an email/phone means either cohort can be marketed to.

The highest-leverage next step is **identity and channel reconciliation**, not a larger blast. Of the 195 phone-bearing first-purchase prospects, 185 match the same Omnisend contact by email and phone, six match by email without a matching Omnisend phone, and four have email and phone pointing to different contacts. **Vici uses Omnisend for email only and LUKO for SMS.** Omnisend's `nonSubscribed` SMS status on 185 clean matches is therefore not a veto on documented LUKO/WordPress SMS consent. Conversely, an Omnisend email subscription or WooCommerce account is not proof that a customer checked Vici's optional SMS box. LUKO currently holds latest evidenced promotional opt-in for 92 of the 195. The earlier LUKO audit also found 181 with unknown/stale DND clearance and no current commercial-clear row. **The existing spreadsheet is not a send-ready audience or a full checkbox-evidence export.** Re-read the canonical WordPress consent metadata per customer, reconcile it into LUKO without inventing grants, and apply current suppressions. The private, row-level verification file is on the Desktop; do not copy its PII into this research library.

## Research-backed hypotheses to test, only after channel/product clearance

### First-purchase prospects

- Use **Woo paid orders** as the definitive exit: if a person purchases, leave every first-purchase sequence immediately. Also pause on support issues, active cart recovery, replies, unsubscribes, or identity ambiguity. Omnisend's [automation exit conditions](https://support.omnisend.com/en/articles/3873110-set-up-exit-conditions-for-automations) support order-based exits on its side; LUKO must enforce the same rule in its own journeys.
- Divide by actual channel permission: email-subscribed prospects may be considered for a carefully reviewed email journey; SMS requires separate, evidenced SMS consent and current LUKO/provider clearance. In the 195-phone subset, Omnisend shows 97 email `subscribed`, 95 `nonSubscribed`, and three `unsubscribed`; those statuses must not be flattened into “reachable.” [Omnisend's own guidance](https://support.omnisend.com/en/articles/3529761-understand-sms-opt-in-methods) treats email and SMS as separate choices.
- **Hypotheses, not proven copy:** answer a first-purchase obstacle (checkout/payment, shipping, COA lookup, product availability), ask what prevented the purchase, and offer truthful assistance. If a discount is tested, compare it with a no-discount service version and account for gross margin and coupon leakage. Do not use dosing, treatment, stack recommendations, deceptive scarcity, or “I saw you browsing” claims without accurate evidence and reviewed permissions.
- Start with a **small, low-frequency, reviewed experiment**, not a mass campaign. Omnisend's [2026 platform report](https://www.omnisend.com/resources/reports/) reports higher observed revenue per behavior-triggered SMS than one-off campaigns, but that is platform-attributed observational data, not a causal Vici forecast. Existing LUKO [cadence policy](/Users/ghost/telynx-inbox/lib/campaigns/cadence-policy.js) defaults to 24-hour spacing, two commercial contacts per week, and four in a rolling 30 days; a cross-platform ledger is needed before those limits mean “across LUKO and Omnisend.”

### Existing buyers and VIPs

- VIP treatment should be a **fulfilled service promise**: priority human response, reliable fulfillment, genuinely early stock/news access, straightforward COA and batch-document lookup, and clearly stated offer terms. Do not promise 24/7 one-to-one coaching unless staffed and scoped. No medical/dosing advice or individualized human-use “stack” recommendations should be generated from order history.
- Ask what support or access the customer values, then record and honor preferences. A private line/community is only valuable if staffed, moderated, and private; measure repeat paid orders, gross margin, support resolution, complaints, and renewal/retention rather than message volume.
- Consider a **single-level referral pilot** only after product/channel/FTC review. Test a reward for a verified new customer's completed, non-refunded purchase. Disclose paid relationships and prevent self-referrals. Do not promise ten referrals per VIP or build a multi-level downline on that assumption. [FTC endorsement guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) requires disclosure of material connections, and [FTC MLM guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guidance-concerning-multi-level-marketing) warns against recruitment-driven incentives. A [banking referral study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1509/jm.75.1.46?journalCode=jmxa) found referred customers more valuable in that context, but it cannot set a peptide referral target.

## Operating design for LUKO × Omnisend (research recommendation, not implemented)

1. Keep **WordPress user ID / Woo customer ID** and Woo paid order as canonical identity and purchase facts. Use email as the next match, normalized E.164 phone only when unambiguous. Do not auto-merge the four split identities or auto-fill the six Omnisend phone gaps.
2. Build a **read-only Omnisend email mirror first**: contact ID, email identity, email subscription status, campaign/automation identifiers and email-touch timestamps where the API or reviewed export actually exposes them, source observation time, and explicit identity conflict reason. Encrypt/limit access to PII; never make Omnisend the SMS-consent or order authority. Vici does not use Omnisend to send SMS.
3. Record cross-platform **commercial touches and active journeys** so a LUKO SMS does not arrive immediately after an Omnisend email or after an order. Import only the minimum email metadata actually available (for example campaign ID and send/click timestamps), and mark unknown recipient-level events as unknown rather than inferred. Avoid duplicating message bodies.
4. Reconcile **opt-outs and suppression** conservatively and promptly. A stop/revocation in either platform must block the appropriate channel in both, with provenance; an opt-in must not be inferred or silently created. Omnisend notes that [manual contact imports can alter subscription status](https://support.omnisend.com/en/articles/3838659-import-contacts-from-a-file), so raw CSV import is not a safe synchronization design.
5. Before any promotion, require a human-reviewed segment snapshot, current Woo paid-order exit, fresh consent and DND/suppression checks, approved product/use-case/copy, send-time guard, and test recipient. No campaign should be sent simply because a contact is in both systems.

There is **no live Omnisend integration in the current repo**. The `order_count = 0` segment grammar exists, but segmentation begins from LUKO `sms_contacts` and cannot represent all WordPress/Omnisend nonbuyers without an identity bridge. The app's customer affiliate program and coaching booking flow are also not built; `docs/referrals/REFERRALS-ARCHITECTURE.md` is staff handoff architecture, not customer commissions. This document is indexed research, **not yet injected into a live campaign-generation prompt or deployed as product logic**.

## Measurement contract

For each pilot, pre-register: audience definition and exclusions; channel permissions; offer cost; test and holdout assignment; time window; primary endpoint **incremental paid orders or contribution margin per eligible customer**; guardrails unsubscribe/complaint/support load/refunds; and stop rules. Report direct click attribution separately from incrementality. Never call platform-attributed revenue “recovered” solely because a purchase followed a message. At 195 phone prospects, a single SMS test is underpowered for modest conversion lifts; pool learning over more eligible cohorts/time or use qualitative friction responses without claiming causal revenue.

The [email-frequency field experiment](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6165626) found fewer messages reduced unsubscribes but also short-term purchases in one retailer, illustrating why “send less” and “send more” are both hypotheses to test against lifetime value, not universal laws. Omnisend provides [quiet-hours/frequency tools](https://support.omnisend.com/en/articles/7731269-manage-sms-sending-limits-frequency-quiet-hours); LUKO must also coordinate outside-platform touches.

## Non-negotiable product and copy gates

- [Telnyx's current US/Canada policy](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/14286763-forbidden-messaging-use-cases-in-the-us-and-canada-10dlc-toll-free-and-short-code) prohibits messages promoting certain drugs, substances not legally approved for sale, and unregulated/prohibited supplements, and requires clear consent aligned to the registered use case. Vici must obtain **written, product-and-copy-specific carrier/provider and qualified legal clearance** before assuming promotional peptide SMS is permitted. Consent alone cannot cure a prohibited use case.
- The [FDA's Summit Research Peptides warning](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/summit-research-peptides-695607-12102024) used website/social claims as evidence of human-use intent despite “research use only” labels. [FTC health-product guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) applies to express **and implied** benefit claims, testimonials, and substantiation. Do not turn a “research” guide into personalized medical advice or imply benefits through a product pairing.
- No fake restocks, fabricated urgency, hidden automation, fake “personal” replies, silent opt-ins, or removal of opt-out handling to imitate one-to-one messaging. The earlier transcript recommends some of these; they are recorded as **rejected tactics**, not instructions.
- US-first is not US-only. Non-US recipients require their own consent/jurisdiction review. Preserve account/store and recipient timezone separately.
