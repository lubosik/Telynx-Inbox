Vici Inbox is an iPhone app. Your invitation is accepted inside that app, not in this browser, so there is nothing to fill in on this page.

Open in Vici Inbox

Already installed the app? This opens your invitation in it.

If you do not have the app yet

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store. It is Apple's own app for trying apps that are not on the App Store yet, and it is free.
  2. Install Vici Inbox using the email Apple sent you. Open it, tap the invitation, and TestFlight will install the app.
  3. Tap the link in our invitation email again. With the app installed it will open there, and you can set your password.
Get it on TestFlight

You should also have an email from Apple inviting you to test Vici Inbox. It arrives separately from ours, so look for it in your inbox and in your junk folder.

If there is no email from Apple, ask whoever invited you. They need to add your Apple ID as a tester before you can install anything.
